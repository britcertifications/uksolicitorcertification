Why us

Why use a UK-qualified solicitor in Dubai?

Many UK documents must be certified, attested or witnessed by a UK-qualified solicitor before they are accepted by HM Land Registry, UK banks, HMRC or the Office of the Public Guardian. A MOFA stamp or a Hague apostille will not satisfy these requirements. A UK solicitor is usually the simplest route. If you are a British expat in Dubai, this normally means flying back to the UK or finding a UK qualified solicitor in the region. This is inconvenient and rarely cost effective.

We work with a number of UK-qualified solicitors based in Dubai, regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority of England and Wales. We arrange low cost certification, attestation and witnessing through these solicitors, to the standard your UK paperwork requires, with same-day turnaround in most cases.

Cash on collection. No retainer, no surprises.

Certified True Copies

We arrange for an SRA-registered solicitor to certify that a copy of an original document is a true and accurate reproduction. The copy is signed, dated and stamped by the solicitor.

Common uses: UK mortgage applications, UK bank account opening, HMRC submissions, professional registrations, pension and probate paperwork.

AED 250 per document · AED 1,000 for a Lasting Power of Attorney

Witnessing Document Signings

We arrange for an SRA-registered solicitor to witness the signing of UK documents under standard solicitor protocol, including Lasting Powers of Attorney, affidavits and deeds.

Common uses: Lasting Powers of Attorney (LPAs), affidavits for UK court proceedings, deeds, UK contracts requiring a witness.

AED 250 per signing

Solicitor Attestation / Verification of Identity

We arrange for an SRA-registered solicitor to attest documents that specifically require attestation by a UK-qualified solicitor, most commonly HM Land Registry ID1 forms.

Common uses: HM Land Registry ID1 forms, statutory declarations, HMRC attestations, Companies House filings.

AED 750 for a fully completed ID1 form · AED 250 for other attestations
